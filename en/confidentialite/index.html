1. Who we are

Mindcare is an Algerian digital mental health platform published by Mindlynk. Email: Mindcare.dz@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

When you create and use your account, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Nickname
  • Login credentials (the password is stored in encrypted form)
  • Information needed to create and manage your account

3. Why we use your data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To allow you to log in securely
  • To contact you about your account or our services
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse
  • To comply with our legal obligations

4. Legal basis

We process your data with your consent and to provide the service you request, in accordance with Algerian Law No. 18-07 on the protection of individuals in the processing of personal data.

5. Data retention

Your data is retained while your account is active. When your account is deleted, your data is erased within [30] days, unless legal retention requirements apply.

6. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data (password encryption, secure connections and restricted access). As no system is completely infallible, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You may access or correct your data, request its deletion, withdraw your consent or object to its processing at any time by writing to Mindcare.dz@gmail.com.

8. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your account and data from within the application [or by writing to the email address above].

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the latest update appears at the top of the page, and we will inform you of significant changes.
